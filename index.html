My Story

I didn't fall into this work. I built it.

I started in customer service, learning to stay calm when everyone else's day was on fire. From there I moved into healthcare operations, where I helped launch six startup clinics from the ground up — systems, scheduling, compliance, all of it. Somewhere in between, I found social media: a place where strategy meets storytelling, and I fell for that too.

"I don't just manage tasks. I build brands, tell stories, and create impact."
